## Conclusion

This is an adoption and attribution gap, not a settlement-engine bug. The
reviewer noted that the lowest-volume currently listed facilitator was around
`$2.80`, but did not publish that figure as a formal acceptance threshold.
Raising the example price, self-paying, funding apparent customers, splitting
payments, or manufacturing transfers would make the evidence less credible
and is prohibited.

The next facilitator submission is gated on all of:

1. at least one independently operated public Base resource server;
2. a recipient controlled by that merchant, not the facilitator operator;
3. multiple organic Base settlements from payer histories that are not
   operator-funded canaries;
4. public evidence tying the resource server, recipient, and measured
   settlement transactions together; and
5. the registry's seven-day per-email facilitator cooldown. Because the exact
   July 26 submission time was not retained, August 3, 2026 is the conservative
   earliest resubmission date.

Exceeding the reviewer's approximately `$2.80` comparison point is preferred
but is not sufficient by itself and is not a reason to alter prices. NEAR
settlements remain useful interoperability evidence but do not satisfy this
registry's current Base measurement gate.
